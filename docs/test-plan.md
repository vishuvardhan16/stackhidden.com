# Test plan

What the automated suite in `tests/` protects, module by module. Run it all with `scripts/run-tests.sh`; CI runs the same suite on every push. Every browser module shares `tests/helpers.py`, which loads the page (from the local files, or from `BASE_URL` in CI), collects every page error and console error, and fails the test if any appear. A silent script error can therefore never pass unnoticed, whatever the module was checking.

## test_static

The owner's standing content rules, checked against the source files with no browser, so this is the fastest and most stable part of the suite. It asserts that the word "Senior" appears nowhere in the site sources, that no portrait file exists or is referenced, that the social preview points at the designed share card, that the charset is declared within the first 1024 bytes, and that the published counts hold: exactly four case files, ten Amazon experience points, 48 skill chips. It also keeps Karyna Naminas credited on both Label Your Data slides, as a byline on the date line (since Nov 6, 2026, when the note under the carousel went), and on no other slide, checks that the Featured quotes are text behind "Read my quote" with no image but the caption logos, and guards that the removed hero-flow diagram stays removed. Since the v5 third pass it also checks the files around the page: robots.txt allows everything and names the sitemap; the sitemap lists exactly the canonical URL; 404.html is noindex, uses only absolute URLs, links only to sections that exist, and uses the site's exact theme colours; the theme-color tags agree with app.js; the home-screen icon is a 180px PNG. And it pins the loading rules: every <img> is sized, lazy, and async, every referenced asset exists (in the markup, the background video's data-src names, and every url() in the stylesheet), and the hero video is preload="none" with no autoplay. Since Nov 8, 2026 it also pins the hero background's markup and files: the video has no src, no autoplay, and no controls until app.js decides, names exactly its four files, and sits in an aria-hidden layer; each video stays within its budget (1.1MB wide, 450KB narrow) with its index ahead of its frames, so it can start playing early, and each still is a WebP under 50KB. Copy edits are the most common change to this site, and this module is what makes a careless one fail loudly instead of quietly breaking a rule.

## test_theme

The dual theme system. The default theme renders, the toggle flips `data-theme`, and the choice survives a reload, because the persistence is the part a visitor actually feels: get it wrong and a returning visitor gets a flash of the wrong theme or loses their setting.

## test_palette

The command palette. Ctrl+K opens it with its commands populated, and Escape closes it. The palette is the page's keyboard front door; if it opens empty or traps focus, keyboard visitors lose the fastest route around the site.

## test_cases

The four engineering case files. Each one opens and closes in place, and the `#case=icm` deep link opens only the ICM file. Deep links are shared and bookmarked, so the wrong file opening is a public bug, not a private one.

## test_carousel

The Featured quote carousel. The dots track the active slide, including `aria-current` on the active dot, and the next button advances. The carousel carries six expert quotes, and its state is what a screen reader announces; the visible slide and the announced slide must agree. Since Nov 6, 2026, when the slides went headline first at the owner's request, it also pins that shape: every slide leads with its headline, linked to the article its quote cites; no slide carries a picture of its quote or repeats his name and title; every quote starts closed and is word for word as published; "Read my quote" opens and closes it, by mouse and by keyboard; Tab reaches only the showing slide; leaving a slide closes its quote and the stage settles back; with motion on, an open quote holds its slide and the carousel moves on once it closes; and a printed page carries every quote, then restores the page exactly. Each of these failed against a matching mutation before it was trusted.

## test_explorer

The interactive lineage explorer. It has exactly nine nodes, and hovering a stage lights only its connected edges. The explorer is the site's signature interactive piece and the visual form of the owner's research; a wiring mistake here misstates the architecture it exists to explain. Since Nov 7, 2026 it also pins the drawing, in the graph's own units so it holds at every width: no two stages within 20 units of each other; every label inside its box with 8 units of room, in the font the machine renders and in DejaVu Sans, the widest common system font; every edge starting and ending on the two stages its id names and staying 12 units clear of every other stage; even margins inside the frame; and the amber packet ending in Flagged anomalies without crossing the Data lake. A recorded bad record, followed frame by frame, never enters the lake, the API, or analytics, and a clean one never touches Flagged anomalies. On the code before the fix these failed on all of it: Flagged anomalies sat 10 units deep on the Data lake with its label against its border, the gate's edge stopped 15 units short of the lake, the dashed edge, the amber packet, and a bad record all crossed the lake, and the clickstream edge grazed Advertiser analytics.

## test_layout

Responsive behaviour at 360px, 768px, and 1400px. At each width there is no horizontal overflow, measured on both the document and the body width (the body check is the one that caught a real bug: a long GitHub link clipped outside its card on phone widths, hidden from the document metric). The data hero figure stays visible and inside the viewport, and the Karyna Naminas byline on the first Featured slide is present, and inside its slide, at every width.

## test_motion

Motion, and its absence. The cursor bot's pupils move toward the pointer; the animated lineage core keeps drawing new frames; the footer motion toggle puts the bot to sleep; and with reduced motion emulated, the core stills and the bot sleeps. Reduced motion is a promise the site makes in its own copy, and this module is what keeps the promise honest in both directions: things move when they should, and stop when they must.

## test_regressions

Bugs that shipped silently before v5 (November 2026), one test each. A shared `var nodes` let the lineage explorer overwrite the background canvas's particle array, so the background painted nothing until a resize and the explorer threw after one; the scrollspy selector matched no links; Contact never lit up at the bottom of the page; the footer Reduce motion control did not stop the carousel; the hero core stayed frozen after motion was turned back on; and every toggle stacked another canvas loop. Each test was run against the pre-v5 code and failed there before it passed on the fix, so it guards the real failure rather than a guess at it. A later addition guards the Featured carousel: choosing the slide that is already showing (its own dot, or a Trace from the query console) used to slide it away and leave the stage empty. Another guards the phone link row, at 320px and 390px (Nov 7, 2026): wherever the row cuts a link, that edge fades and the cut part lies inside the fade; the lit link sits clear of both fades; a swipe comes to rest on a link, not mid-word; widening the window to desktop leaves the desktop row as a fresh load draws it; and back at the top from Contact no link is lit and the row starts over, which is also checked on desktop. Before the fix the left edge cut "ting" of Writing with no fade, and Contact stayed lit at the top. Another guards in-page jumps: About, Writing, and Query, at 1400px and 390px, must land within 30px below the sticky header. Each jump starts from a fresh load, not a reload: a reload restores the previous jump's scroll position, and on a busy machine that restore could land after the click (about 1 run in 6 failed under load until Nov 7, 2026). On the code before the fix all six landed under it, and Writing's heading ended above the viewport. None of these bugs threw at load or changed how the page looked in a screenshot, which is why they need tests to stay fixed.

## test_query

The Query this page console. Its central promise is that its tables are the page, so the first tests check that against the DOM directly: every table's row count equals the number of cards, slides, spans, roles, case files, and skill chips on the page, and titles and dates read back exactly as published. The rest pin what a reader relies on: grouping and sorting, NULL semantics (IS NULL finds the current role, = NULL never matches and says why), case-insensitive LIKE, and LIMIT; error messages that explain and suggest ("titel" suggests "title"); visitor input that is never rendered as HTML, checked with an image-tag payload; Trace outlining the right element, bringing a quote's carousel slide forward, and Back returning focus to the Trace button; Tab completing a table name and otherwise moving focus so the keyboard is never trapped; history restoring an unsent draft; the palette running SQL; the query kept in the URL; a #q= link running on arrival; and at 360px, wide results scrolling inside their own box with dates kept on one line and the header no taller than 100px.

## test_records

Send a record through the lineage explorer. The feature makes one claim, that a bad record never reaches anything past the quality gate, and the module checks it from the pipeline log rather than from the animation: a clean record's log runs ingest, transform, gate, lake, api, analytics; a bad record's stops at gate then anomaly, with no lake line and both lines marked failed. Two records in flight at once keep their own routes. Under reduced motion the record arrives at once with no moving dot. At 360px the graph's labels stay at least 10px tall and the graph scrolls inside its wrapper without widening the page.

## test_animations

The three v5 animations: the hero intro, the 2026 trace render, and the lineage draw-in. Each must play when motion is allowed, never play when it is not, and leave the page exactly as it would be without it. So the module checks that the hero animates; that the trace arms below the fold, renders on first view, and then drops every class and custom property it added; that the graph arms, draws, and then carries no inline declarations, keeps its dashed anomaly edge, and still traces on hover; that turning motion off mid-draw finishes the drawing at once; that a visitor arriving by a #lineage link always ends with a fully drawn graph (waiting for the outcome, since the browser smooth-scrolls to the anchor first); that the head script applies a stored Reduce motion choice before app.js runs; and that under reduced motion nothing is armed or animated. It also pins the trace's accuracy: every bar width and month label is recomputed from its date on the Jul 1 to Dec 1 axis, because a bar that encodes a date is only worth animating if it ends on the right one.

## test_publishers

The publisher mastheads and the Featured carousel's sizing. Every writing card, talk card, and featured caption opens with a publisher name, a kind pill, and a logo slot; empty slots stay collapsed and the page requests no logo file; a logo, once added, replaces the name visually while the name stays in the text, and in dark mode sits on a white plate (checked with a neutral grey placeholder, not any real mark). Writing runs in date order, and the closing links of the first row share a baseline. The query console's writing, talks, and featured tables read their outlet, venue, and title from the mastheads. Last, at 360, 768, and 1400px no caption on any slide runs under the carousel's arrows and dots; run against the pre-fix code, that check fails at 360px by about 50px. Since Nov 6, 2026 the same holds for every slide's quote when it is open. With the official logos in place, it also checks the logo system in both themes (bringing each section into view first, since logos load lazily): every visible logo loads and has its own size line (--h); the mastheads of cards side by side are exactly the section's logo box tall and their dates sit on one line; in dark mode every chip is as tall as the logo box and white or Marketplace Risk red; and at 360px no kind pill runs past its card. Run against the logo commits as first pushed, the alignment checks fail in both sections and both themes, and the phone check catches the clipped "Conference talk" pill.

## test_career

The career trace at the top of Experience, in both themes. A bar that encodes dates is only worth showing if it ends on the right dates, so every span's position and width are recomputed in Python from its start and end months, with the current role ending exactly at today's mark, and every year label and the "now" label sit where their dates put them. Every bar must be painted at the track's full height: the bars first used a class named "role", which the page's older .role heading rule matched, and its margins collapsed both role bars to a quarter of a pixel while their numbers were still right. Each row must restate its entry below, organisation, title, and dates, in that entry's words. The keys work (arrows, Home, End, Enter), and the detail line gives the LinkedIn-style length and the entry's place, invents no place for the degree, and links to an entry that lands below the header. From 360px to 1400px no year label touches another or "now". The first-view render plays, then removes everything it added; under reduced motion the spans are drawn in place. With JavaScript off the inline positions are the whole chart, so they are checked against the same computation as of Nov 2, 2026. The position, length, label-fit, and painted-bar checks each failed against a matching mutation before they were trusted.

## test_finishing

The skip link, the theme colour, the home-screen icon, and the 404 page. The skip link is off screen until the first Tab, then fully visible above the header, and Enter moves focus to main. Both theme-color tags follow the theme showing, whichever control changed it. The icon loads at 180 by 180. The 404 page runs against a small local server that behaves like GitHub Pages for a missing address (404.html, with status 404, at any depth): the query line restates the path, every link and icon works from a deep path, single quotes are doubled as in SQL, markup in the path stays text (checked with an image-tag payload and its side effect), a long path is cut at 80 characters, the saved theme wins over the OS setting, and the page fits 360px. Chrome logs a console error for the document's own 404, which is the page working; the module drops exactly that message, by URL, and any other failed load still fails the test.

## test_background

The hero background (Nov 8, 2026), the looping lineage network behind the top of the page. A visitor gets the file for their theme and width and nothing else: the dark or light 1920x1080 file on desktop, the narrow one at 390px, and only that file's still. The still is not even asked for until app.js has arrived (checked with app.js held back 600ms over HTTP), so it never shares a slow phone's bandwidth with it; then it fades in, and fades in again after a theme flip picks the other still. Without JavaScript the dark still shows at once, at both sizes, and no video is asked for. Under reduced motion, a stored Reduce motion choice, Data Saver, or slow-2g, no video downloads at all, even across a theme flip, and the still shows, at once, with no fade. A theme flip hides the old video at once, in the same instant, so the new still never sits under the old picture, and then plays the other file; with motion off it downloads nothing new until motion comes back. A late playing event from a file that no longer matches is ignored. Crossing 920px swaps to the narrow file and back. The video pauses when the layer scrolls off screen and when the tab is hidden, and resumes after. The layer runs from above the top of the page and edge to edge without widening it, covers the whole hero on desktop and a band about one screen tall on a phone, and sits in the page's stack (z-index -1 in a hero that is not a stacking context). It is aria-hidden, muted, unfocusable, and ignores the pointer, and it is gone in print and under forced colours. Last, the hero's text keeps WCAG AA contrast over the still in both themes at 1400 and 390px, read pixel by pixel in the page itself, since CI installs no image library. These checks assert requests, src values, and play and pause calls, never decoded frames, and they pass with every video replaced by bytes no decoder accepts. Each was run against a matching mutation (30 in all, from dropping the Data Saver check to loading the still with the stylesheet) and failed there before it was trusted. One more mutation, a dark narrow tint of 0.30, passed: the text still clears AA there, so it is not a break.

## test_loading

What the page downloads, and when, on desktop and at 390px. No logo or screenshot loads with the page; logos arrive as their section comes near; within a screen of the carousel every caption logo loads, off-stage slides included, so no slide ever slides in blank; the carousel asks for no quote image at all, even with every slide shown and every quote open (the quotes are text since Nov 6, 2026); and the carousel's height does not change as the logos arrive. The hero loop is requested when motion is allowed, and never under reduced motion, Data Saver, or a slow-2g connection, where the poster stays. Five of these checks failed against a matching mutation before they were trusted: eager logos, no carousel preload, preload="metadata", no Data Saver check, and no declared sizes.
