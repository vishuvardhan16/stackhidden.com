# Content Rules

The rules the owner set for what appears on this site. They are not style preferences. Follow them on every edit.

## No photo

No photograph of the owner anywhere on the site: not in the hero, not in social preview tags, not inside screenshots used on the page. The quote screenshots in the Featured carousel were rendered with the avatar hidden for this reason. His portrait file stays off the repo entirely.

## Two records, kept separate

The site presents two bodies of work and does not blend them.

1. **Amazon.** The job: role, team, experience, and the four case files. This is the engineering record.
2. **In his own name.** The independent record: research, articles in the trade press, the conference talk, and expert quotes. A divider section, "In My Own Name", opens this part of the page.

Do not frame the independent work as Amazon output, and do not frame the Amazon work as personal research. The About section names the separation plainly, and edits should keep it that way.

## Never publish

- His level.
- His office or building.
- His manager's name.
- Internal package or repository names. Program and system names that are already public on the page are fine.
- His Outlook address. It is for journal publishing only and stays off the site. Contact on the site is his Gmail and LinkedIn.

## Titles

He is a Software Development Engineer, a software engineer. Never call him a data engineer in site copy. His data work is part of his software engineering role, and the copy says so.

## Voice

Short, plain, and in his words where he has given them. His tech stack line is his own sentence: Back-End Engineering, Java, APIs, and Big Data. Numbers on the page come from his record and are stated the way he stated them, approximate where he said approximate. If a number is not in his record, it does not go on the page.
